Tap the screen, add a ROM file you own, and play. Every game you add stays on
your shelf in this browser, along with its saves, so next time it's one tap
and you're right where you left off. Hit **MENU** for three save slots,
fast forward, and backing up your save file.

**On iPhone:** open the [full-screen Prash Boy](/prashboy/), tap Share, then
**Add to Home Screen**. It opens like an app with no browser bars, and Safari
won't clear your games and saves the way it can for websites you haven't
visited in a while. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen.
